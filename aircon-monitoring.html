ABOUT THE BUILD

WHAT IT DOES

The Aircon Monitoring App helps organize buildings, air-conditioning units, maintenance schedules, service history and equipment status in one Excel-based application.

FEATURES

KEY FUNCTIONS

Building and location management
Aircon unit inventory
Maintenance history tracking
Cleaning and service scheduling
Equipment status monitoring
Inventory and maintenance reports

GETTING STARTED

HOW TO USE

01

Download the App

Click the Download App button and save the workbook to your computer.

02

Open in Microsoft Excel

Open the downloaded .xlsm file using Microsoft Excel.

03

Enable Macros

If prompted by Excel, enable the required workbook content so the application can run.

04

Register Your Units

Add buildings and air-conditioning units, then begin recording maintenance activities.
